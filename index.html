My dearest Mark,

Happy birthday, baby. ❤️ If I could give you one thing today, it would be the chance to see yourself through my eyes, so you could understand how incredibly special you are to me.

You spend your days finding your way across the sea, but you will always be one of my favorite places to come home to. I admire your strength, your courage, and the way you keep moving toward the horizon. I hope this new year brings you safe voyages, calm waters, good health, success, and every dream your heart is carrying.

I wish I could be beside you today—to wrap my arms around you, steal a kiss, and celebrate the wonderful man you are. Until I can, let this little corner of the internet carry a piece of my heart to you. No distance is big enough to make you any less loved.

So here's to another year of adventures, blue water, beautiful sunsets, and memories we haven't made yet. Wherever your ship takes you, remember there's someone cheering for you and counting down to the next time she gets to hold you.

Happy birthday, my handsome captain. I love you beyond the horizon. 💙

Always yours, your girl ♡
